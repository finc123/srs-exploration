How we count AI subscribers from e-receipts

Gross adds, churn, net adds and the subscriber index for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity and Muse, in the US (NIQ Slice) and Europe (NIQ Fox: UK, DE, FR, ES, IT). In one line: gross adds from everyone; churn rates from the inboxes we trust; subscribers = last month + adds − rate × last month.

2 · Gross adds

  1. Keep paid receipts only. Each provider's filter keeps priced charges, such as “ChatGPT Plus (Monthly) $19.99” from Apple, and drops welcome emails, unpriced confirmations, trials, credits and business plans. One receipt per member × provider × channel × day.
  2. Line up each member's receipts by date. A subscription starts at the first receipt, and again after any gap longer than the lapse window W (see section 3).
  3. Gross add = the first paid receipt of a subscription, counted in the month it lands. A payment after a cancellation email also starts a new subscription (one churn, then one gross add).

Gross adds use every panellist on every visible channel. Welcome emails are never counted as adds: only 11% of direct Perplexity welcomes and 54% of Google One AI welcomes are followed by a payment.

3 · Churn

  1. A subscription churns when no paid receipt arrives within the window W after the last one. W is the 99th-percentile gap between consecutive renewals, measured per provider × channel (40–91 days for monthly plans; 135 for quarterly, 400 for annual). Churn is dated when W runs out, not backdated.
  2. Cancellation emails don't date churn. Even with every NIQ table combined, they cover only 56–88% of real lapses (Apple 62%, Google Play 75–84%, Claude direct 57%), and in Europe there are almost none.
  3. Problem: missed renewal emails look like churn. On some channels the panel misses renewal receipts. Apple captures about 94% for ChatGPT, so about 1 in 16 is missing. One missing monthly receipt opens a gap of about 60 days, longer than W, so a subscriber who is still paying is counted as churned.
  4. Fix: measure churn rates only where emails are reliable.
    • full panel Channels capturing ≥ 97.5% of renewals (ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini on Google Play; Claude direct; EU Claude Stripe) use everyone.
    • reliable subset Everything else, and Apple always, uses only members whose inbox received another regular subscription email that same month (iCloud+, YouTube Premium, Spotify, or another AI app). That proves the inbox was working, so a missing AI receipt is a real stop. In this group Apple captures 99.1% of renewals.
    • The check also covers the months after the last payment. A single month without a check email is tolerated if the months either side have one. Members whose inbox stops showing them are dropped from the rate, not counted as churn.
  5. Split into buckets and compute each bucket's rate every month: provider × channel × plan × tenure (1st month, 2–3, 4–6, 7–12, 13+). Rate = churns detected in the month ÷ subscriptions live at the start of the month, in the trusted population. Buckets with under 100 live subscriptions borrow the rate of a broader group.

Tenure matters most: new subscribers churn at 15–24% a month, long-standing ones at 2–4%. The rates are applied to all subscribers in each bucket in section 4.

Lapse windows and rate source:

Capture = share of expected renewal receipts seen, for all members and within the reliable subset. Where a cell lacks enough renewal gaps, its window is pooled across the provider's channels.

4 · Subscribers, net adds, share

churn(t) = Σ over buckets [ subscribers in bucket at end of t−1 × bucket's churn rate in t ]
subscribers(t) = subscribers(t−1) + gross adds(t) − churn(t)
net adds(t) = gross adds(t) − churn(t)
  • Subscribers (modelled) is the headline. It is built cohort by cohort from January 2024, starting from the observed stock.
  • Observed subscribers: simply counting open subscriptions in the full panel. Shown as a check. It sits below the modelled count wherever missed Apple emails drop live subscribers.
  • Share = a provider's subscribers ÷ the total across tracked providers. Per 1,000 = subscribers ÷ active panel members × 1,000.
  • Held month: if the check emails behind the reliable subset haven't arrived yet, that month shows gross adds only (in October 2026, the current month).

5 · Worked example: ChatGPT, US, September 2026

  1. Receipts. Apple and Google Play ChatGPT charges. Direct chatgpt.com is excluded (sign-up receipts only, no renewals).
  2. Windows. Apple 42 days, Google Play 40 days. Example member: Apple Plus receipts on 29 Jan, 29 Feb, 29 Mar and 29 Apr 2024, then nothing until 31 Oct 2024. The gap of 185 days is more than 42, so their first subscription churned on 10 Jun 2024 (29 Apr + 42 days) and a new one, a gross add, started on 31 Oct.

7 · Caveats

  • Channels receipts can't see: direct ChatGPT (excluded; ChatGPT understated), Grok/X web, Perplexity web, Muse web (Meta Pay). The card panel sees web billing; it is kept separate.
  • The reliable subset is slightly optimistic: its first-renewal rate is about 4 points above everyone else's. This is disclosed, not corrected.
  • Apple can't be validated directly. The observed Apple count loses about 6% of renewals to missed emails, so modelled and observed differ most for Apple-heavy providers (ChatGPT, Grok, Perplexity).
  • Grok churn lags 2–3 months because of its long windows.
  • Gemini includes AI Plus 2 TB (storage-bundled) from late January 2026. A split without it is planned.
  • Open NIQ questions: the May–June 2026 drop in Google One storage receipts, the mid-2025 step in Apple cancellation emails, and the fall from July 2025 in direct ChatGPT sign-up receipts.
