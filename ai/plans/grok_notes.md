# Grok (xAI) / X Premium+ – notes

**Descriptors**
- X Corp: `X CORP. PAID FEATURES` (often with +14156978699), legacy `TWITTER ONLINE` / `TWITTER ONLINE ADS*TWTR DUBLIN` (ads, not subs), `GOOGLE *X CORP. PAID FEATURES` (Play billing). Basic, Premium, Premium+, Verified Orgs all share the same descriptor, so tier must come from amount.
- xAI / SuperGrok: `XAI LLC`, `XAI LLC PALO ALTO`, `X.AI`, `Grok AI Assis CA`. API credits go through Stripe and use similar xAI text. Exclude API/Business by amount (e.g. $5+ top-ups, $30 x seats).

**VAT**: X lists EU/UK prices ex-VAT. DE checks out at €19.00 (later €24.99) and FR at €19.20/€201.60, so card amounts differ by country (DE 19%, FR/UK 20%, ES 21%, IT 22%). Treatment for the UK is assumed, not confirmed. SuperGrok appears to be billed in USD in Europe (not confirmed), so EU/UK card amounts follow the exchange rate.

**Collisions**
- SuperGrok annual ($300) falls between the Premium+ annual prices ($229 and $395). Monthly US prices don't overlap. Use the descriptor (X Corp vs xAI) to separate them.
- Premium+ UK: the help centre says £31 ex-VAT (£37.20 incl. VAT), while TechCrunch reported £35. Use a £35–38 band.
- EU Premium €9.52–9.76 and Basic €3.57–3.66 are clearly separate from Premium+ (€19+).
- Spanish press quoted Premium €8.47 and Basic €3.23, which suggests a lower ES base. Unverified.
- FR press reported Premium+ "€37.70 → €57" from 2025-03-22. This is probably App Store pricing. Unverified.

**Price-change timing**: For the Dec 2024 hike, new subscribers paid more from 12-21 and existing subscribers from the first bill after 2025-01-20. For the Feb 2025 hike, US prices changed 02-18 and other markets 02-19, with existing subscribers moving from about 03-22. Expect blended amounts during these transitions.

**Uncertainties**: the UK Premium+ annual price for Dec 2024–Feb 2025, the launch dates and annual prices for SuperGrok Lite and Plus, and when SuperGrok became available in the EU/UK (grok.com geo-blocked the EU/UK at launch). The Wayback Machine and help.x.com could not be fetched (403/blocked).

**Promos**: SuperGrok cancel-flow offer of 3 months for $30; Heavy at 3 months for $99; X annual-plan discounts; India price cuts in July 2025 (not in scope). Heavy includes Premium+.
