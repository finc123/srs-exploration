Card Data Sources Guide

What each card and bank transaction dataset in Snowflake is, how the datasets relate to each other, and which one to use for a given analysis. Written for the Muse agentic-transaction work, but it covers every live row-level source in the Earnest, Fable, SHARE_CE, SHARE_EARNEST and SHARE_FABLE databases.

Fin Creedon · 16 Oct 2026 · Figures measured in Snowflake 15–16 Oct 2026 · Working files in exploration/card_datasets_inv/

Bottom line

  1. Every live US source comes from Consumer Edge (which absorbed Earnest), and most of it from Yodlee. The tables are different cuts and enrichments of two underlying panels, Omega and Tau. They are not independent samples, so never add them together.
  2. Prime Omega (2822) is the superset for the Omega panel. It contains about 99% of the Yodlee agentic transactions Muse finds today, plus about 44% more from sources that Muse doesn't read. Despite being labelled per-merchant, its MERCHDESC keeps the full statement text, LINKAGNT* included.
  3. Tau is a separate panel that Muse can't see today. Tau adds about 440 agentic transactions a month, on top of about 1,040 in Omega (September 2026).
  4. For Muse, the recommendation is to move from the raw Yodlee feeds to Prime Omega. Either use Josh's view (EARNEST.COMMON.CE_PRIME_OMEGA_PANEL_TXNS), or read TRANS_V1_0 joined to MID_V1_0 directly for the full population. Treat Tau as a separately reported panel.
  5. Drop the old sources: 279 DIRECT* and SIGMA, and Yodlee feeds 1 and 2 (all stopped), and every snapshot, change-log and mirror copy. Use 279 only where its Earnest merchant tags or existing pipelines are needed. It is being replaced by 2822.

How the sources fit together

All the US data is Consumer Edge. Raw card and bank data comes from Yodlee aggregation. Consumer Edge then delivers it in three forms: raw, Earnest-enriched (the legacy "279" schema), and CE-enriched (the new "2822" schema). Fable is a separate European vendor.

Prime Omega (2822) use this

SHARE_CE_PRIME_ENRICHED_OMEGA_2822.PRIME_OMEGA.TRANS_V1_0 + MID_V1_0, MID_MATCH_V1_0, CARD_V1_0, CARDLINK_PANEL_V1_0

Consumer Edge's new production row-level schema ("Orion Prime 2.0"), and the replacement for 279. It is the whole Omega panel: 156B rows, 12.9 TB, and about 54M transactions a day.

Ids
TRANSID (CE id); PARTNER_TRANSID = Yodlee transaction id = 279 PARTNER_TRANSACTION_ID; CARDLINKID = Yodlee UNIQUE_MEM_ID = 279 MEMBER_ID (all measured)
Description
MERCHID → MID_V1_0.MERCHDESC. This is documented as a merchant descriptor, but it is the statement text with dates and card digits stripped, so it works as a per-transaction description (see Agentic descriptors).
Dates
TRANS_DATE (cleaned; use this), PARTNER_TRANS_DATE, PARTNER_POST_DATE, FILEDATE
Panels
22 panels in CARDLINK_PANEL_V1_0. CE recommends aggregate_consistent_shopper_2022_v2 (≈3.6M cards), or _2019_v2 (≈0.5M) for longer history.
Watch for
It is unclustered: always filter FILEDATE as well as TRANS_DATE. Exclude PRIME_OMEGA_STATIC.AFFECTED_ACCOUNTS_092026. Our role can't read PRIME_TAU inside this database, so use the TAU_2822 database for Tau.

Josh's view: CE_PRIME_OMEGA_PANEL_TXNS use for panel work

EARNEST.COMMON.CE_PRIME_OMEGA_PANEL_TXNS (view; DDL in srs scripts/sql/earnest_ce_prime_omega_panel_txns_view.sql)

Prime Omega restricted to cards in the two consistent-shopper panels. Merchant description, brand, CER symbol, channel, card, income, home location and panel weights are all joined on, from 2019-01-01. It excludes the September 2026 affected accounts. The panels cover about 22% of Omega's transactions (418M of 1,939M in Dec 2025).

Grain
One row per transaction per matching ticker symbol. Count DISTINCT TRANSID rather than rows.
Use
Filter weight_agg_cs2022 IS NOT NULL, weight by the same column, and add currency = 'USD'. Always filter TRANS_DATE, and FILEDATE too.
Watch for
It is slow: about 2.5 minutes per symbol-week, and an unfiltered MIN/MAX doesn't finish. A filter on SYMBOL drops untagged merchants, including Link agent descriptors. The all-cardholder sibling CE_PRIME_OMEGA_TXNS is on srs master (PR #2522) but isn't deployed. The view doesn't apply the user-score exclusion used in the 279→2822 validation SQL.

Prime Tau (2822) separate panel

SHARE_CE_PRIME_ENRICHED_TAU_2822.PRIME_TAU.TRANS_V1_0 + MID_V1_0, MID_MATCH_V1_0, CARD_V1_0

A separately sourced panel. It shares no transaction or member ids with Omega or Yodlee, and has about 27M transactions a day. CE describes it as "completely separate from Omega", with no recommended member panel. The schema is the same as Omega's.

Description
MERCHDESC, as for Omega
Panels
There is no CARDLINK_PANEL table, so SRS would have to build its own panel.
Watch for
The history start is unclear: rows from 2013, against "Jan 2024" per CE and 2023-03-28 in 279 PRIME_TAU. It was dropped from Muse on 7 Oct 2026 because it was small (about 3 agentic transactions a day in 279) and would distort the per-million denominator.

Yodlee feeds 3, 4 and 6 what Muse reads today

SHARE_CE_SPEND_201_PRIME_EX_TAU.FEED{3,4,6}_PANELS.FEED{n}_{CARD,BANK}_PANELS_V1_0

Raw, unenriched Yodlee transactions with the financial institution's own description text. Together they make up about 40% of Omega. The three feeds are separate panels: no transaction ids repeat across feeds and almost no members do. Within a feed, card and bank tables share members (31% of feed 3 card members and 57% of feed 6 card members are also in the bank table).

Ids
UNIQUE_{CARD|BANK}_TRANSACTION_ID, UNIQUE_MEM_ID
Description
DESCRIPTION, the raw FI text
Watch for
It is unclustered and prunes only on PANEL_FILE_CREATED_DATE. Pending and posted copies of one charge can carry different ids. There are no panels or weights, and the account and refresh tables are incomplete. Feeds 1 and 2 ended (last transactions May 2025, last update 15 Sep 2025). No production pipeline reads these feeds; only Muse does.

279 PRIME and PRIME_TAU legacy schema

SHARE_EARNEST_279.{PRIME,PRIME_TAU}.SPEND_279_TRANSACTIONS_V3_0 (the SHARE_CE_SPEND_279_* databases are exact mirrors)

Earnest-enriched transactions in the legacy schema, with Earnest merchant tags (PARENT_MERCHANT, MERCHANT, category, channel). PRIME is a subset of Yodlee feeds 3/4/6: about a third of feed 3 plus parts of 4 and 6. PRIME_TAU is a 43% subset of Tau.

Ids
EARNEST_TRANSACTION_ID, PARTNER_TRANSACTION_ID (= Yodlee and Omega partner id), MEMBER_ID
Description
BILLING_DESCRIPTION, per transaction
Panels
PRIME.MEMBER_PANELS_V3_0 and PRIME_STATIC.MEMBER_WEIGHTS_V1_0. Use the _v2 methods; the legacy ones still exist and still feed company_data.
Watch for
Rows repeat once per merchant tag (about 1.4 rows per transaction; Link agent rows carry both link_agent_wallet and the real merchant), so count distinct ids. 2822 replaces this schema. The DIRECT* and SIGMA schemas stopped in 2025.

EARNEST.<TICKER>.TXNS company work

EARNEST.{PDD,UBER,NFLX,SPOT,ETSY,CHWY,DIS,WBD,AIRLINES,RBLX,TSLA,…}.TXNS

SRS-built daily extracts. The sync-service earnest-<TICKER> jobs load the monthly 279 PRIME snapshot from Earnest's S3 bucket, filtered by a merchant regex. They apply the daily change logs, then add SCALARS, the CS 2019/2022 flags and a v2 WEIGHT. Measured: PDD.TXNS is entirely inside 279 PRIME. Despite its name it covers PDD, Shein, Amazon, Walmart, Etsy, Chewy, TikTok and Ulta: about 12% of 279's transactions.

Use
Ticker KPI work where the weights and scalars are already applied.
Watch for
These are pre-filtered to named merchants, so they can't find new merchants. Several schemas are stale (DASH, META, TMUS, LAD, LMND, ROKU). A member with several v2 weights can fan out rows, so compare COUNT(DISTINCT earnest_transaction_id) with COUNT(*).

Fable (Europe) Europe only

SHARE_FABLE.FABLE_SIGNAL.{UK,DE,ES,IT}_V2_TXN, FR_V2_TXN, FR_V3_TXN (+ _SUPP, USER_MASTER, REFERENCE_DATA)

A separate vendor covering European credit, debit and open-banking panels, one table per country, with a per-transaction DESCRIPTION. Dec 2025: FR v3 71M, FR v2 55M, UK 53M, ES 26M, IT 13M, DE 11M. The *_SIGNAL views equal the *_TXN tables.

France
FR v3 (debit) replaces FR v2 (credit), which is removed by 31 Dec 2026. The two use different keys. v3 has about 2.5× the users, and about 65% of v2's rows match v3 on description and amount.
Watch for
There are no weights on rows; Fable uses a rolling-panel method. SRS's FABLE.COMMON aggregates come from the separate Signal Daily shares, not from these tables. Muse found no LINKAGNT* rows in Fable, only regular link.com* wallet rows.

Not covered here

  • Snapshot, change-log and backup copies (PRIME_SNAPSHOTS*, PRIME_INCREMENTAL*, *_BKP). Snapshots match their current tables to within 0.1%.
  • Mirror databases (the four 279 shares, OMEGA/TAU_2822, the three Fable and three TRANSACT_USA databases).
  • Consumer Edge TRANSACT_USA and the *_DAILY_SIGNAL shares. These are CE's pre-aggregated KPI products (no transaction rows): the "official" daily and period KPIs by symbol, brand and geography.

Agentic descriptors: can 2822 replace the Yodlee feeds?

Muse finds Stripe Link agent payments by matching LINKAGNT*, or LINK* with www.link.co / south san fra, in Yodlee's DESCRIPTION. 2822 has no per-transaction description column, so the question was whether its per-merchant MERCHDESC keeps that text. Every test below covers September 2026.

Yes. MERCHDESC keeps the Link agent text. Of Muse's 739 agentic transactions, 730 are in Prime Omega (by partner id), and all 730 match the same agentic pattern on MERCHDESC. The text is the Yodlee description with dates, card digits and bank prefixes stripped. For example, Yodlee's LINK* STARBUCKS 09-28 WWW.LINK.COM CA 9376 DEBIT CARD PURCHA becomes LINK* STARBUCKS WWW.LINK.COM CA.

Combining sources. Omega and Tau are separate panels, so their raw counts can be added. Their per-member rates should be weighted separately, because Tau has no panel. Never add 279 to 2822, Yodlee to Omega, or a ticker table to 279: each pair is a subset of the same panel.

Suggestions for jjsinger and cwang

  • Move Muse's card source from the raw Yodlee feeds to Prime Omega. It contains 99% of today's agentic transactions plus about 44% more, it's a production share, and it puts Muse on the same base as the rest of the team. Use the full Omega population for counts and merchant mix, and Josh's panels for weighted rates. In September the panels' agentic rate (0.70 per million) matched the rest of Omega (0.68), but they hold only 262 events a month.
  • Deploy the all-cardholder CE_PRIME_OMEGA_TXNS view (PR #2522). Descriptor-led work needs every cardholder, not just the consistent-shopper panels.
  • Document that MERCHDESC works as a per-transaction description. Its column comment calls it a merchant description, which suggests 2822 can't support descriptor work. It can.
  • Agree how to treat Tau. It adds about 440 agentic transactions a month and has no panel. The options are to report it as a separate series, to build a simple consistent-shopper panel for it, or to exclude it.
  • Add date-pruning guidance to the view. Always filter FILEDATE together with TRANS_DATE, because the tables are unclustered and an unfiltered MIN/MAX doesn't finish.
  • Retire the stale ticker schemas (DASH, META, TMUS, LAD, LMND, ROKU TXNS), and move company_data to the v2 weights.
  • Rotate the Earnest S3 keys. They are in plaintext in a Notion page and in the sync-service configs.

Method and files

All files are in exploration/card_datasets_inv/:

  • card_datasets_inventory.ipynb plus results/inventory.csv and inventory.xlsx: every table and view in the 18 reachable databases (3,404 objects), with date range, description column and December 2025 distinct transactions.
  • overlap_sample.py and overlap_analyse.py → results/overlap/: the hashed one-day samples and the pairwise overlap matrices (pairs.csv).
  • merchdesc_test*.py → results/merchdesc_test.txt: the agentic descriptor tests.
  • docs_collected.md: the existing documentation, with sources (Notion, Slack, srs, sync-service and Snowflake comments), each claim tagged as stated, inferred or measured.
