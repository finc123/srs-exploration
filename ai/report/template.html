AI subscribers in card panels

Paid subscribers to eight AI providers in the Consumer Edge US panel and five Fable European panels, built from web and Google Play card charges. A subscriber needs 35 days of panel tenure before their first payment, lapses with no renewal within 45 days of a monthly charge (365 + 35 for annual), dated one month after the last payment, and is removed from history entirely if they leave the panel.

The latest month is partial. Churn for the last ~45 days, recent gross adds and panel exits are provisional and will be revised as renewals and late data arrive.
