# Current-gen console install base (PS5 + Xbox Series X|S): for GTA VI pre-orders as % of base

Compiled 2026-10-27. Sources: Sony results (via press), Take-Two investor decks and calls (srs_documents / Quartr), broker notes (AlphaSense GenSearch), Circana (Mat Piscatella, via press), VGChartz estimates, and Yipit / M Science emails.

Terms used below:
- **Sell-in** means units shipped to retail (Sony's reported number).
- **Sell-through** means units sold to consumers (Circana, VGChartz).
- **Units** are console counts, not households or users.

## 2. Management statements (item 4)

**Take-Two (Zelnick).** He has given no PS5/XS install-base number in any call from Q2 FY26 to the 2026 AGM.
- TD Cowen, 27 May 2026: "if there are eight billion people and you've got two or three billion phones, and you've got an install base of console of under 200 million, it does not mean you're going to 10x the market".
- Q4 FY26 call, 21 May 2026. An analyst (Sebastian) suggested the current-gen base "may not be as high … as it might otherwise have been". Zelnick replied: "when we look at pricing, I would not say we look at it in the context of the install base."
- Q1 FY27 call, 7 Aug 2026. On pre-orders: "our pre-orders are exceptional. No one's ever seen anything like this before at Take-Two or in the industry"; "we haven't sold one unit yet. You can cancel a pre-order"; "the level of pre-orders is unprecedented and astonishing … we just don't know how it'll translate into sales." On streaming: it "can 10x the effective installed base"; "we see the overall installed base as a practical matter growing materially."
- AGM, 17 Sep 2026: "It's a single-player title and it's being launched on Xbox and PS5."
- The decks (see rows 9 and 10) are the only quantified Take-Two figures: about 127m Gen 9 consoles at Dec 2025 and about 129m at Mar 2026.

**Sony.** Gives cumulative PS5 sell-in and PS MAU only (rows 1–5). No search turned up a GTA VI-specific install-base comment from Sony or Microsoft. Microsoft does not disclose Xbox Series units.

## 3. Recommended install base

### Assumptions

**Global PS5.**
- 25 Jun 2026: use sell-through of about 93.3m (VGChartz Jul LTD of 94.09m minus 0.78m sold in July). Sell-in is about 95.2m.
- ~18 Oct 2026: VGChartz end-Aug is about 94.9m (130.24m combined minus about 35.3m XS). Adding about 0.9m for September and about 0.45m for 1–18 Oct gives about 96.3m.
- Sell-in at 30 Sep has not been reported. If Jul–Sep shipments are 2.2–3.2m (the prior year was 3.9m), sell-in at 30 Sep would be about 97.5–98.5m.

**Global XS.**
- About 35m: Take-Two-implied 35.3m at Mar 2026, VGChartz 35.1m at end-Jun, and roughly +0.1m a month since.
- The range is 26.4m (Morgan Stanley) to 37m (Welfare).

**US PS5.**
- Circana: 26.88m at Sep 2025. VGChartz: 29.56m at Apr 2026, about +0.2–0.25m a month since.
- Circana YTD through August is −25%, but September was +22%.
- Cross-check: the US share of global PS5 is about 32%, consistent with Circana's 26.9m against about 82m global sell-through at Sep 2025.

**US XS.**
- About 80–85% of the VGChartz Americas figure of 21.9m (the US PS5 share of Americas is about 81%), which gives about 17.5–18.5m. The undated 17.05m figure supports the low end.
- I use 17.5m for the central case.

**Units to households (US).**
- About 3–5% of units retired, broken or exported: roughly 2m of 47.5m.
- Dual PS5 + XS households: about 25–35% of XS owners also own a PS5, which is about 4.5–6m. This is a judgement; I found no 2026 survey figure.
- Same-household PS5 duplicates (PS5 Pro upgraders keeping the old unit, second consoles): about 1–2m.
- In total, units overstate households by about 15–22%.
- US households are about 133m (Census).

For a mailbox- or household-like panel, the US household figure (about 39–40m) is the right denominator for "% of base that pre-ordered". Use units (47.5–48.7m) only if comparing against unit sales.

GTA VI is console-only at launch (PS5/XS; no PC date), so PS4/Xbox One and Switch 2 are excluded. The PS MAU figure of 125m includes PS4 and counts accounts, so do not use it as the denominator.

## 4. Yipit and M Science: do they give pre-orders as % of install base? (item 5)

**Neither gives pre-orders as % of install base.** I searched Outlook from June to October 2026 for "install base", "installed base", "penetration" and "attach" with GTA/console. The phrase queries returned no hits. The "Take-Two install base" query matched only Yipit Weekly Tracker volumes and M Science digests on other words, and none of those uses the term.

**Yipit, Take-Two Weekly Tracker Vol. 17 (22 Oct 2026).** Gives units only, with no install-base framing:
- "We estimate preorder unit sell-through at 4.9m (thru 10/18) … extrapolates to 26m units opening weekend (thru 11/22), 43m units at F3Q27 quarter-end (December), and 45m units at F4Q27 quarter-end (March). This excludes PC."
- The 4.9m is scaled from a US e-receipt panel (digital only) against Battlefield 6's reported (global) units.
- Earlier volumes: 3.7m (Vol. 14, through 9/27) and 3.85m (Vol. 15, through 10/04).
- Implied by me, not Yipit: 4.9m against a global base of about 132m is about 3.7%. 43m by December is about 33%.

**M Science.** The emails are link-outs to the portal. The visible text has no install-base or penetration metric:
- TTWO FQ2 '27 Preview (19 Oct 2026): "We continue to see data supporting an outlook for as many as 50mm units". GTA VI presales have "outpaced Modern Warfare II by 3.2x in the U.S. and Europe data combined".
- Sunday Sauce (25 Oct 2026): "the data still supports the possibility of as many as 50 million units through the December quarter." Also: "roughly 22% of U.S. GTA VI pre-purchasers have historically spent on Roblox" (an overlap figure, not penetration).
- Implied: 50m against about 132m is about 38%.
- The full PDFs on portal.mscience.com were not accessible (the M Science MCP is not authenticated), so a chart-level install-base reference cannot be ruled out.

**Brokers that do frame it as % of base** (via AlphaSense):
- UBS (13 Oct 2026): "Our F27 unit estimates imply ~30% sell through amongst the current Playstation 5 and Xbox Series X/S global console base". Its survey found about 85–90% purchase intent among PS5/XS owners.
- BofA (27 Oct 2026): Gen 9 "Lifetime Penetration" of 33.2% in FY27, rising to 70.4%. That is 45m first-year units on a base of about 138m.
- JPMorgan (21 Sep 2026): Take-Two guidance embeds about 34m FY units, which is about 25–28% of the console base. Its US survey found 71% intend to buy and 39% have pre-ordered or plan to.
- Morgan Stanley (23 Oct 2026), citing Sensor Tower: digital pre-orders of about 6.1m as of 13 Oct 2026.

These broker figures come from AlphaSense GenSearch summaries. Verify the exact wording in the PDFs before quoting them externally.

## Caveats
- Microsoft does not disclose Xbox Series units, so every XS figure is a third-party estimate (26–37m globally).
- Sony's Jul–Sep 2026 shipments were not yet reported as of 27 Oct, so the October global PS5 figure is an estimate.
- No Circana US PS5 cumulative figure later than Sep 2025 surfaced, and no Circana US XS cumulative figure surfaced at all. US numbers after Sep 2025 rest on VGChartz plus Circana's Y/Y trends.
- The household dedupe ratios are judgement calls. A 2026 survey of dual ownership (Circana PlayerPulse, Ampere Consumer) would firm them up if one is available.
