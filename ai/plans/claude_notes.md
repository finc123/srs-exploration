# Claude (Anthropic) – amount-band notes

**Descriptors.** Consumer web: `CLAUDE.AI SUBSCRIPTION` (to ~Jun 2026), then `ANTHROPIC* CLAUDE SUB`. Other reported forms: `ANTHROPIC`, `ANTHROPIC CA`, `ANTHROPIC* SAN FRANCISCO CA`, `ANTHROPIC ANTHROPIC.COM CA`, `ANTHROPIC +14152360599 US`, `ANTHROPIC PBC`, `CLAUDE AI`, `CLAU AI PRO PLAN`. B2B: `ANTHROPIC CLAUDE TEAM +14152360599 US`. API credits and usage top-ups most likely post as plain `ANTHROPIC`; I found no API-specific or EU-specific descriptor. App-store subscriptions post as `APPLE.COM/BILL` or Google and should be excluded.

**Amount collisions.**
- **Pro annual = Max 20x monthly** everywhere from 2025-04-09: $200 / £180 / €180 net (€214.20 in DE). Tell them apart by recurrence.
- $100 can be Max 5x or a win-back half-price Max 20x first month. $50 can be a win-back Max 5x. $10 (€10.80 FR) is the Pro 3-month promo.
- $125 = Team premium seat (2026). $150 = 5 Team seats or 1 premium seat (2025).

**Atypical amounts.**
- Upgrades bill prorated: a full cycle of the new plan minus unused value of the old one. Monthly-to-annual switches carry a credit.
- Usage credits are arbitrary top-ups with their own receipt, so one card can show two charges a month.
- US state sales tax is added in some states (Pro about $21–22).
- Some UK/EU subscribers stay on legacy USD billing ($20 + VAT, e.g. $23.80 in DE). Panels would show these as FX-converted, non-round amounts.
- Businesses with a VAT ID pay net (€18 / £15).

**Uncertainties.**
- UK/EU Pro annual price for Nov 2024–Apr 2025 is assumed to be 0.9× the $216 USD price.
- GBP/EUR Max start dates are assumed to equal Max launch.
- Promo windows are approximate.
- Some secondary sources claim UK is billed in USD.
- Team GBP/EUR prices after 2026 are not verified.
- Wayback shows the Team price cut on about 2026-01-28. Blogs that date it Sep 2026 are wrong.
