Meta Muse

Launch tracker for Muse (released ): app usage and marketing, web traffic, subscriptions and agentic checkout. Faint bars are daily values; lines are the trailing 7-day mean (TR7D).

Methodology

Card panels and e-receipts see different halves of the market and are never added together: cards see web sign-ups billed by Meta, e-receipts see App Store and Google Play subscriptions.
