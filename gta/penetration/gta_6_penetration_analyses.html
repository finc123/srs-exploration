GTA VI pre-order penetration of console owners

1. Cumulative % of console owners who have pre-ordered or bought: GTA VI vs Battlefield 6

Aligned on days to launch: day 0 is 19 Nov 2026 for GTA VI (solid lines) and 10 Oct 2025 for Battlefield 6 (dashed lines). Each line starts on the day its pre-orders opened: day -147 (25 Jun 2026) for GTA VI and day -71 (31 Jul 2025) for Battlefield 6. For each game the base is active-panel mailboxes that joined before its pre-order open and bought a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S console before then. Click a legend item to hide or show its line.

  • Purchases: first console base-game order per mailbox at any store (PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store and retailers). Gross: cancellations are not netted. Battlefield 6 excludes PC (Steam, EA app, Epic), the open beta, add-ons and in-game currency.
  • "PS5 owners: PS5 version" counts PS5 owners buying the PS5 edition (PlayStation Store or a retail PS5 SKU), and likewise for Xbox. Mailboxes that own both consoles appear in both bases.
  • Left of the launch line both games are pre-orders; Battlefield 6's line continues past launch with post-launch sales. GTA VI pre-orders opened 76 days earlier in the cycle than Battlefield 6's.
  • About 29% of explicit owners never show a PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store receipt, so their digital purchases are invisible and both curves understate true penetration. Walmart pre-orders are not parsed. Counts are unweighted.
