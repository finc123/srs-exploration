# ChatGPT (OpenAI): notes for amount bands

**Descriptors.** Web charges (Stripe) show as `OPENAI *CHATGPT SUBSCR`, `CHATGPT SUBSCRIPTION +14158799686 US/CA`, `OPENAI *CHATGPT` or `OPENAI SAN FRANCISCO`. A bare `OPENAI +14158799686` usually means API credits, which should be excluded. Team and Business probably share the CHATGPT descriptor. App-store charges show as `APPLE.COM/BILL` or `GOOGLE *ChatGPT` (form inferred). iOS prices differ from web: US Plus $19.99, EU €22.99, UK Go £6.99.

**Currency switch (main trap).** Before about Feb 2025, UK and EU web subscriptions were billed in USD plus VAT: UK $24, DE $23.80, FR $24, ES $24.20, IT $24.40. The card shows those as converted GBP or EUR amounts that move with FX. After the switch, prices are flat and include VAT: £20 / €23 for Plus and £200 / €229 for Pro. Existing subscribers stay in USD until they resubscribe, so legacy amounts carrying FX noise continue. The switch date is approximate.

**Plans.** Plus $20 (Feb 2023). Pro $200 (5 Dec 2024). Go $8: EU €7.99 from late Dec 2025, global launch 16 Jan 2026. Pro 100 (9 Apr 2026). Pro 500 (29 Sep 2026). There are no annual consumer plans. EUR prices are the same across DE/FR/ES/IT. US prices exclude sales tax, so allow up to about +10.5%.

**Uncertain.** UK web prices for Go (£8?), Pro 100 (~£89–100) and Pro 500, and EU web prices for Pro 500. These come from iOS prices or secondary sites. EUR and GBP Business prices after April 2026 are estimates.
