# Perplexity consumer plans: notes (as of 2026-10-19)

**Plans.** Pro costs $20/mo or $200/yr, unchanged since before 2023. Max launched 2025-07-02 at $200/mo or $2,000/yr. Education Pro costs $10/mo (SheerID-verified). The 2026 pricing page leads with $17/mo and $167/mo "billed annually". Some trackers cite $204/yr for Pro, but $200/yr is confirmed as of Feb 2026, so check this against panel amounts. Non-consumer plans: Enterprise Pro at $40/seat/mo or $400/yr (launched 2024-04-23), Enterprise Max at $325/seat/mo or $3,250/yr (launched about Aug 2025), and an edu/non-profit Enterprise rate of $30/seat.

**Currency/VAT.** Web billing is in **USD everywhere**, with no local-currency web prices found. For EU/UK, VAT appears to be added on top of the USD base. A German Magenta account renews at **$23.80** (= $20 + 19%). That implies UK/FR $24.00, ES $24.20 and IT $24.40 per month, and 10x those figures for annual. Only DE is directly confirmed. In Fable (GBP/EUR), amounts will be FX-converted and drift over time, so build bands per day from USD x FX. Allow about +/-3% for card FX fees. App Store prices (e.g. GBP 20, EUR 22) are a separate channel and do not appear as Perplexity card charges.

**Atypical amounts.** A $10 first month (referral credit). $10 for 3 months (50% retention offer at cancel). Multiples of $40 for Enterprise seats.

**Free distribution (suppresses card charges for about 12 months, then a conversion cohort follows):**
- US: Xfinity (2024 to Aug 2025); PayPal/Venmo (2025-09-03 to 2025-12-31)
- DE: Telekom Magenta (from 2025-01-29)
- ES: Movistar (from 2025-01-24)
- FR: Bouygues (2025-02-11 to 2026-02-11)

- Outside our regions: Airtel (India) and SoftBank (Japan)

**Descriptors.** WWW.PERPLEXITY.AI; PERPLEXITY.AI +15102700840 (CA/US); PERPLEXITY.AI SAN FRANCISCO CA; "Perplexity* Trial Over". Stripe suffixes such as "PRO 5102700840 CA", "MAX +15102700840" and "ENT PRO +15102700840 CA" may identify the plan directly.
