# Muse from Meta: consumer paid plans (as of 2026-10-19)

**Availability.** US from 2026-09-08 (SensorTower shows a partial day on 09-07); Canada from 2026-09-18. **Not launched in the UK or any EU market (DE/FR/ES/IT)**, and no date has been announced. Fable Europe panels should therefore show no genuine Muse subscriptions; any hits are VPN users, expats or noise.

**Plans.** Monthly only: no annual option and no annual discount. No free trial; the only promo found is invite-code bonus tokens (1B), not a price discount.
- Power: $20/mo in-app (500M tokens/wk); **$16/mo on the web** (direct Meta).
- Maximum: $100/mo in-app (3B tokens/wk); **$80/mo on the web**.
- Press quotes $20/$100. The web prices ($16/$80, before sales tax) come from our own card data (METAPAY META.COM charges) and a few outlets that mention a ~20% web discount. No Meta page states them.
- Canada: CAD 28 Power (one outlet, plus $28 iTunes receipts); CAD 139 Maximum not verified.

**Price changes.** None since launch. Muse Wonder (2026-10-12) is a model, not a consumer plan. It comes with **Muse Code** plans ($5/$15/$50/$200), a separate coding product (consumer=N in the CSV). The app "gets Wonder soon, subscribers first", with no price change announced.

**Descriptors.**
- Direct web: `METAPAY META.COM <ST>` (no `*`). `METAPAY*` is P2P/other. The same descriptor carried legacy $11.99/$12.99 charges before launch, so use price bands $15.99-17.60 and $79.99-88.00.
- Apple: "Muse from Meta Power/Maximum Plan".
- Google Play: GOOGLE *FACEBOOK (cannot be separated from other Meta apps).

**Watch-outs.** The pre-existing Meta One Core ($7.99) and Premium ($19.99) plans are a different product. The Premium price sits next to the $20 Power price.
