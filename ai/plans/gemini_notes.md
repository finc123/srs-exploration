# Gemini (Google One AI tiers): notes

**Descriptors.** All billing posts as Google One: `GOOGLE *Google One g.co/helppay#`, and also `GOOGLE *Google Storage`. There is no Gemini-specific descriptor. iOS purchases appear as `APPLE.COM/BILL`. Exclude `GEMINI TRUST CO` (crypto), `GOOGLE *CLOUD` (API) and `GOOGLE*GSUITE` (Workspace).

**AI vs storage collisions:**
- **US:** $19.99 AI Pro monthly = 100GB annual. $99.99 Ultra-5x (from 2026-05-19) = 2TB annual = 20TB monthly. $249.99 Ultra (2025-05 to 2026-05) = 5TB annual. $199.99 AI Pro annual = Ultra-20x.
- **UK:** £79.99 Ultra-5x = 2TB annual. £189.99 AI Pro annual = Ultra-20x.
- **EU:** €99.99 Ultra-5x = 2TB annual. €219.99 AI Pro annual = Ultra-20x.
- Separate them by the gap between charges.

**Break.** From 2026-01-27 the legacy 2TB plan ($9.99 / £7.99 / €9.99) includes AI Plus, and it is now labelled "Google AI Plus (2 TB)". The price did not change. Marked consumer=N (ambiguous).

**Promos:**
- AI Plus intro: $3.99 / £3.49 / €3.99 for 2 months.
- Ultra intro: $124.99 / £119.99 / €139.99 for 3 months.
- AI Pro annual promo: $99.99 (Dec-2025 to Jan-2026).
- $0 periods: free trials, student free years (UK/EU accounts convert from about autumn 2026), and Pixel and Galaxy bundles.
- Price cuts apply at the next renewal, so old and new prices overlap.

**Tax.** US prices exclude tax (taxing states add 0 to 10%). UK and EU prices include VAT and are the same across DE, FR, ES and IT.

**Uncertainties.** Not verified: UK annual and 5TB storage prices, EU 5TB, EU and UK start dates (assumed to match the US), and US and UK AI Plus annual prices. Wayback evidence is mostly US. EU and UK prices come from occasional captures and the live pages.
