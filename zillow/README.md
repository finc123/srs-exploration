# Zillow (Z): Aug–Oct 2026 check-in

Run from this folder with the shared `..\.venv`. Each notebook is generated by its `build_0X_*.py` script, then executed in place:

```powershell
..\.venv\Scripts\python.exe build_01_mortgage.py
..\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m jupyter nbconvert --to notebook --execute --inplace 01_mortgage.ipynb
```

| Notebook | Signal | Benchmark (`data/va_benchmarks.csv`, from Visible Alpha via SRS MCP) |
|---|---|---|
| `01_mortgage.ipynb` | Cotality Z loan volume (`BOTS.Z.LOAN_VOLUME_*`), loan officers | Total / purchase origination volume, mortgage revenue |
| `02_showcase.ipynb` | 72degree listings (`BOTS.Z.LISTINGS_AGG`, `LISTING_DETAILS`, `LISTINGS_V3`) | Other Residential and Premier Agent revenue (proxy only) |
| `03_traffic.ipynb` | SimilarWeb `VISITS_V2` + SensorTower `CORE` (Zillow Group web + apps) | Visits, average monthly unique users |

`common.py` holds the shared helpers: Snowflake `q()`, `va()` to load the benchmarks, and `backtest_stats()`. Charts go to `output/`.

## Backtest summary (out-of-sample)

| Signal | Benchmark | MAPE | Consensus MAPE | Beat/miss hit rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cotality Z volume ÷ T4Q capture rate | Total originations | 0.9% (17 qtrs) | 13.1% | 13/13 |
| Group web visits + app sessions | Visits | 4.7% (14 qtrs) | 6.7% | 13/14 |
| Main-app DAU | Monthly unique users | 3.1% | 2.0% | 71% (directional only) |
| Showcase growth | Other Residential revenue | 4.7% (5 qtrs) | 5.1% | 40% (not predictive) |

The mortgage backtest uses fully filled-in Cotality data. Weekly snapshots only exist from 2026-08-07, so a live nowcast is less accurate than 0.9% (see the fill curve in 01).

01 replays Q3-23, Q3-24 and Q3-25 at today's fill level (Jul 98.8%, Aug 94.2%, Sep 54% visible):
- **Fill-adjusted:** errors of −3.9% to +3.3%.
- **Unadjusted:** errors of −17% to −20%. This is the method the `cotality_loan_volume_backtest` dashboard uses, which is why its Q3-26 nowcast reads +24.5% YoY ($1.59B).

## Q3-26 read (as of 2026-10-12)

- **Originations:** about $1.90B (range $1.83–1.97B) vs consensus $2.27B (−16%). Growth slowed from +71% YoY in Jul to +48% in Aug and about +26% in Sep (Sep is provisional). Zillow's share of total market volume is flat at about 0.8%.
- **Visits:** about 2.29B (range 2.18–2.40B) vs consensus 2.58B. Web is +4–7% YoY and the app is −16% to −24% YoY; most of the gap comes from a tough 2025 app comparison across the whole category. zillow.com gained share of US portal web visits (+2.7pp YoY).
- **Showcase:** 63.2k active listings (2.59% of for-sale + pending), +94% YoY. The base has been flat since July because new Showcase adds stopped growing while exits held steady, so it is levelling off around 66k. Opendoor is ramping; Howard Hanna and Redfin are pulling back.
