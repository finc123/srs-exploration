# Cursor (Anysphere) - pricing notes (as of 2026-10-19)

**Currency/tax:** Cursor bills through Stripe in **USD in every market**. Listed prices exclude tax. UK and EU cards see a USD charge converted at the card network's FX rate (plus any foreign-transaction fee), so local-currency amounts drift with FX. From about **Jan-Feb 2026**, forum reports show **VAT added on top** for UK and EU customers: Pro $20 becomes $24.00 in the UK/FR, $23.80 in DE, $24.20 in ES and $24.40 in IT. Before that, most EU/UK users paid the bare USD amount. The CSV splits rows at 2026-01-01, which is an approximate date.

**Timeline**
- 2023 to Jun 2025: Pro $20/mo or $192/yr; Business $40/seat/mo or $384/yr.
- 2025-05-06: free Pro year for verified students. Closed to new applicants 2026-06-25. Expect first $20 charges as these years end.
- 2025-06-16: Ultra launched at $200/mo. Pro moved to a $20 usage pool. Unexpected charges from 16 Jun to 4 Jul were refunded.
- ~Jul 2025: Pro+ launched at $60/mo. Annual billing is reportedly not offered on Pro+ or Ultra.
- ~Mid-2025: Business renamed Teams. ~Jul 2026: Teams Premium seat at $120.
- 2026-08-14: SpaceX completed its acquisition of Cursor.
- **2026-10-12: The X Subscription** (Grok + Cursor + X Premium): Plus $8 ($80/yr), Premium $30 ($300/yr), Super $100 ($1,000/yr), Ultra $200. The legacy Pro, Pro+ and Ultra plans are closed to new signups, but existing subscribers can keep them. New-subscriber amounts and possibly descriptors (X/xAI?) will change. Verify in the panels.

**Overage:** usage is billed at API rates as separate mid-cycle invoices. These start at about $20 of outstanding usage, the threshold rises with payment history, and invoices are capped at about $100. **A $20.00 usage invoice is identical in amount to Pro**, so use the descriptor to tell them apart.

**Descriptors seen:** `CURSOR, AI POWERED IDE`, `CURSOR, AI POWERED IDE NEW YORK`, `Cursor Ai Powered Ide`, `CURSOR USAGE OCT NEW YORK USA` (usage, with the month abbreviation). Anysphere may appear on invoices.

**Low confidence:** Pro and Business launch dates before 2025, the exact Pro+ date, the VAT start date, the Teams Premium seat, X Subscription annual prices and descriptors, and US sales tax.
